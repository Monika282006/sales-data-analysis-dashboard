# Data Analytics – Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data and developing an interactive sales dashboard using Microsoft Power BI. The project demonstrates the complete data analytics process, including data preparation, analysis, visualization, dashboard development, and presentation of business insights.

The dashboard is designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance using Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), charts, filters, and interactive visualizations. It helps users understand sales trends, profitability, product category performance, regional performance, sales representative performance, and sales channel performance.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* To understand and analyze the given sales dataset.
* To clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
* To identify important sales and profitability metrics.
* To calculate and display important Key Performance Indicators.
* To analyze monthly sales trends.
* To compare sales performance across product categories.
* To analyze sales performance across different regions.
* To evaluate profit generated by sales representatives.
* To compare different sales channels.
* To create an interactive and visually appealing Power BI dashboard.
* To present meaningful business insights through data visualization.

## Data Cleaning

Before performing the analysis, the dataset was reviewed and prepared for visualization.

The data preparation process included:

* Checking for missing or blank values.
* Identifying duplicate records.
* Checking column names and data structure.
* Verifying appropriate data types.
* Checking date fields and ensuring they are recognized correctly.
* Reviewing numerical fields such as sales, profit, and quantity.
* Checking categorical fields such as product category, region, sales representative, and sales channel.
* Ensuring the data was suitable for analysis in Power BI.

After the data preparation process, the dataset was used for dashboard development and visualization.

## Dashboard

The main deliverable of this project is an interactive sales dashboard created using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance through KPI cards, charts, and slicers.

The dashboard includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Quantity Sold
* Profit Margin
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales by Product Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Sales Representative
* Sales by Sales Channel
* Interactive slicers for filtering the dashboard

The Power BI dashboard file is available in the `Dashboard` folder.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard contains four major KPIs:

### Total Sales

Displays the overall sales amount generated from the available sales records.

### Total Profit

Shows the total profit generated from the sales transactions.

### Quantity Sold

Represents the total number of products or units sold.

### Profit Margin

Shows the percentage of profit generated relative to sales.

These KPIs provide a quick summary of the overall business performance.

---

## Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following major visualizations:

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart is used to analyze how sales change over time.

This visualization helps identify:

* Increasing sales trends
* Decreasing sales trends
* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Overall sales patterns

### 2. Sales by Product Category

This visualization compares sales performance across different product categories.

It helps identify which categories contribute the most to overall sales.

### 3. Sales by Region

This visualization compares sales performance across different geographical regions.

It helps identify strong and weak-performing regions.

### 4. Profit by Sales Representative

This visualization compares the profit generated by individual sales representatives.

It helps identify sales representatives who contribute significantly to profitability.

### 5. Sales by Sales Channel

This visualization compares sales generated through different sales channels.

It helps understand which channel contributes the most to overall sales.
